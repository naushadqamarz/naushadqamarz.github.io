Parking Spots: NSW Stations privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Parking Spots: NSW Stations is an Android app and an iOS app, made by NqApps. The Android package name and the iOS bundle identifier are com.nqapps.parkingspots. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Accounts

NqApps has no accounts and does not store personal information on its own servers. Third-party services used by the app process data as described below.

Google AdMob

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process the advertising ID, IP address, and device and diagnostic/usage data under Google's privacy policy and Google's AdMob policies. The app requests non-personalised ads only (no ad personalisation), and does not ask for or use Apple's App Tracking Transparency tracking permission.

Remove ads

An optional monthly Remove ads subscription is billed through Google Play Billing on Android and through Apple In-App Purchase on iOS. Payment details are handled by Google and Apple. NqApps never sees them. Purchase status is kept on the device.

OpenStreetMap

Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Those requests reveal the device IP address and the map area viewed to OpenStreetMap's servers. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Transport for NSW

Requests to the Transport for NSW Open Data API for car park data do not include the user's location or any user id.

Contains data from Transport for NSW, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Parking Spots: NSW Stations is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Transport for NSW or the NSW Government.

Location

Location is used on the device only, to sort nearby car parks and open directions. It is never sent to NqApps or to Transport for NSW.

What stays on the device

Favourites, settings, cached car park data, and alert preferences are stored on the device only.

Retention and deletion

NqApps stores nothing about users on its servers, so there is nothing to delete there. Data held by Google (ads, billing, and Play), Apple (App Store and In-App Purchase), or OpenStreetMap is governed by their policies and controls. You can reset the advertising ID in device settings and cancel subscriptions in Google Play or Apple subscriptions. Uninstalling the app removes on-device data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
