Parking Spots privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Parking Spots is an Android app and an iOS app on the App Store, made by NqApps. On the App Store the app is named NSW Train Car Parking. The Android package name and the iOS bundle identifier are com.nqapps.parkingspots. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Accounts and personal data

There are no accounts. NqApps does not collect personal data.

What stays on the device

Favourites, alerts, and settings are stored on the device.

Location

Your location is used on the device only, to show nearby car parks and distance. It is not sent to our servers or stored by us.

Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, the advertising ID, and usage data under Google's privacy policy. On iOS, you can control tracking in Apple's settings.

Remove ads

An optional monthly "Remove ads" subscription is billed through Google Play Billing on Android and through Apple In-App Purchase on iOS. We do not see or store payment details.

Car park data

Car park data comes from Transport for NSW.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
