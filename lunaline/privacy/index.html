Lunaline privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026.

Lunaline is published by NqApps, Sydney. The game runs on your device. There is no Lunaline account and no Lunaline server. Lunaline is distributed on Google Play and on the Apple App Store. The Android package and the iOS bundle id are both com.nqapp.lunaline.

Data stored on the device

Progress, settings, streak and the remove-ads flag are stored in app preferences on the device. On Android, backup and device transfer can include those preferences so an update or a new phone can restore them. On iOS, the same preferences stay in the app's local storage. Uninstalling Lunaline deletes that on-device data. NqApps does not receive a copy of your progress.

Advertising

The free download shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home and level screens, an interstitial between some completed puzzles, and an optional rewarded ad when you ask for an extra hint. Ads are not shown during a puzzle, at app open, or in the tutorial, and they are capped as described in the store listing. Ad requests use a PG content rating.

AdMob may process the IP address (which can estimate a coarse location), device information, advertising data such as which ads were shown, and ad interactions, under Google's policies. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may also send diagnostic information such as crash and performance data so the SDK can operate.

On iOS, Lunaline requests non-personalised ads. The app does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. Where the operating system provides an advertising identifier, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still receive it as described in Google's policies. It is not used by Lunaline to track you.

On Android, AdMob may use the advertising ID. You can reset that ID in system settings.

In the EEA and the UK, a consent form lets you choose whether ads are personalised. On iOS the ad request remains non-personalised even after that form. Settings includes ad privacy options when Google requires them.

A one-time purchase, Remove ads, turns off banners and interstitials and makes hints available without an ad.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the store you use.

On Google Play the product id is remove_ads. Google Play processes the payment. NqApps does not receive your card number or other payment details.

On the App Store the product id is lunaline_remove_ads. It is a non-consumable (a one-time purchase, not a subscription). Apple processes the payment through the App Store using In-App Purchase (StoreKit). NqApps does not receive your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Apple provides only the information needed to unlock Remove ads on your devices. You can restore the purchase from Settings, then Restore purchases. Apple keeps its own record of the transaction under Apple's policies. Manage or request Apple's copy through your Apple ID.

The price shown in the store (suggested A$3.99) is set by the store and may differ by country.

Analytics and crash reports

On Android, if analytics is allowed, Firebase Analytics records events such as starting a puzzle, finishing a puzzle, using a hint, and sharing a daily result. Those events include the mode, size, duration and similar gameplay facts. They do not include your name, email, contacts or the puzzle answer.

On Android, Firebase Crashlytics may send a crash log, device model, OS version and a Firebase installation ID so crashes can be fixed. Collection is off in debug builds.

Google processes this data as a service provider. You can refuse analytics storage in the consent form where it is shown.

The iOS app does not start Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. It does not send those gameplay events or Crashlytics reports. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still send its own diagnostic data, described under Advertising.

Data retention

NqApps does not run a Lunaline server and does not keep an account profile.

Progress, settings, streak and the remove-ads flag remain on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

AdMob data is kept by Google for the periods in Google's policies. NqApps does not hold a separate copy.

Store receipts and payment records are kept by Google Play or Apple, not by NqApps.

Where an Android build has Firebase configured, Google keeps analytics events and crash reports under Google's policies. The iOS app does not send those Firebase reports.

How to request deletion

Email sharmnadqam@gmail.com from the address you want us to reply to. Say that you are asking about Lunaline and, if you can, include the platform (iOS or Android).

On-device progress is deleted by uninstalling Lunaline. There is no cloud save to erase.

We will tell Google, as far as we are able, that you want AdMob data associated with your use of Lunaline deleted. We do not hold an identifier that maps you to a particular ad profile.

Apple purchase records are deleted or managed by Apple through your Apple ID. NqApps cannot delete Apple's copy of a receipt.

Google Play purchase records are managed by Google through the account that made the purchase. NqApps cannot delete Google's copy of a receipt.

If you used an Android build that sent Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics data, email the same address and we will pass a deletion request to Google for that project. The iOS app does not send that Firebase data.

Permissions

The app needs a network connection for ads and purchases. On Android it uses the advertising ID and Play billing. It does not request the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or storage. On iOS it does not request permission to track you.
