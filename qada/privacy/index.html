Track Qaza Salah and Fasting privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 (app renamed to Track Qaza Salah and Fasting; formerly "Qada Tracker").

Track Qaza Salah and Fasting ("the app") is an app for estimating and tracking missed (qaza) prayers and fasts. It is made by NqApps, an Australian seller. This policy explains what the app does with information. The app is an estimating tool, not a religious ruling. Please consult a qualified scholar.

Your data stays on your device

The app has no account, no analytics and no cloud sync, and it does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Everything you enter stays only on your device. That includes your madhab and puberty-age settings, the dates you enter, estimated and made-up counts, the dated make-up history used for the progress graph, your language choice, and reminder settings. None of it is sent to NqApps, which has no server. Device backup is turned off for the app (Android). You can export a backup file yourself through your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Daily reminders are scheduled locally on the device, and notification permission is only requested when you turn reminders on.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (Google UMP)

Unless you buy "Remove ads", the app shows banner ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The app requests non-personalised ads. Where required (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent form before ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Even for non-personalised ads, the Google SDK may collect the device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information. Google uses these to serve and measure ads, cap frequency and prevent fraud. Your prayer and fasting data is never shared with Google or anyone else. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

The app is not directed at children.

Remove ads purchase (Google Play Billing / App Store)

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase (A$2.99 in Australia, or the local equivalent) that only removes ads. Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store processes the payment. NqApps does not receive your card details or billing address. The app only checks whether you own the purchase, and Restore re-checks it.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete it with Settings > Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app. NqApps keeps no copies. Data collected by Google is kept under Google's own policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
