This page describes the TickedSets app (package com.nqapps.tickedsets) on Android.

In short

  • Your training log stays on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • NqApps does not collect, receive or sell your workouts or any other personal information. Google collects some device data when ads are shown (see Ads).
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Google's consent tool (UMP) runs before any ad loads.
  • TickedSets Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. It removes ads.
  • Backups are files you export and keep yourself.

On-device data

Workouts, routines, exercise names, notes, settings and the Pro flag are stored only on this device, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no sign-in. TickedSets does not send that data to NqApps or to anyone else. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in the app. Notes you type in the app stay on the device like the rest of your log.

You can delete individual workouts and routines in the app. Uninstalling TickedSets, or clearing its storage in the system settings, deletes all of its data from the device. NqApps holds no copy, so it cannot restore or delete anything on your behalf.

Backups you export

Android cloud backup and device transfer are switched off. To move your log to another device, you export a backup file (or a CSV) from Settings yourself and restore it on the other device. An exported file goes only where you choose to save or share it, and that place's own privacy terms apply. NqApps never receives it.

How long data is kept

Your log stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall TickedSets. NqApps keeps no copy. If you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer it and any follow-up, then delete it. Data that Google's ads SDK collects (see Ads) and Google Play purchase records are kept by Google, not by NqApps. NqApps cannot access or delete them. Google explains how long it keeps data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. For example, it anonymises advertising data in server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Ads

The free version can show ads through Google AdMob. Banners appear on History and Progress only, and not during a workout. At most one interstitial is shown per day, and only after you finish a workout. There is no ad when you open the app and no ad when you leave it. A rewarded ad can unlock advanced charts for 7 days. TickedSets Pro removes ads.

Before ads load, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool runs. Where Google's consent form applies (for example in the UK and EEA), ads are personalised only if you consent to personalised advertising (IAB TCF purposes 3 and 4). Everywhere else, TickedSets requests non-personalised ads. Settings includes Privacy options when Google requires that choice.

Even non-personalised ads involve data collection by Google. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), your interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's advertising partners are listed at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

TickedSets Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. NqApps does not receive your name, email address or card details. The app only asks Google Play whether this store account owns Pro, so Restore purchase works on a new device, and keeps a Pro flag on the device. Purchases and refunds are handled by Google Play.

Notifications

Notifications are used only for the rest timer. You can switch sound and vibration off in the app, and you can refuse or turn off notifications in the system settings.

Who receives data

  • Google AdMob, for ads in the free version, as described above: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play (Android), for the one-time Pro purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

No one else receives data from TickedSets. NqApps itself does not receive, sell or share personal information. The ads and store providers above handle data under their own policies.

Children

TickedSets is not directed at children. The intended audience is 18 and over.
