Privacy Policy

Noorani Qaidah privacy policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026. Last updated: 29 September 2026.

Noorani Qaidah (package com.nad.nooraniqaidah) is a free educational Android app for learning the Quran and Tajweed, in the Pakistani Edition (Indo-Pak script). It is made by NqApps. This policy describes what the app does with information.

What Noorani Qaidah stores

Noorani Qaidah does not ask you to create an account, and there is no sign-in. After install, the lessons work offline. NqApps does not collect or store personal data on any server.

Advertising

The app shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, including the advertising ID and other device IDs, and usage data to serve and measure ads. You can read Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Remove ads

An optional in-app purchase to remove ads is available through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled by Google, and NqApps never sees payment details.

Children

Noorani Qaidah is educational content for children learning the Quran and Tajweed. The app may be used by children with a parent or guardian, and it shows ads through Google AdMob as described above. NqApps is committed to the Google Play Families Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
