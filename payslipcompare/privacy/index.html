Payslip Compare privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Payslip Compare is an Australian take-home pay estimator made by NqApps. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Your inputs stay on the device

Payslip Compare does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. The salary, hours and options you enter, and your settings, are stored only on your device so the calculator can remember them. They are not sent to NqApps, and NqApps has no server. You can clear them at any time in Settings > Clear saved inputs, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (Google UMP)

If you have not bought Pro, the app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Where required (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Google AdMob may collect and share the device advertising ID, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), IP address and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and improve ads and prevent fraud. Your pay inputs are never shared with AdMob. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Payslip Compare is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Pro purchase (Google Play Billing)

Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play Billing that removes ads. Google Play processes the payment; NqApps does not receive your card number or billing address. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Pro.

Retention and deletion

NqApps keeps no copies of your data. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept under Google's own policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
