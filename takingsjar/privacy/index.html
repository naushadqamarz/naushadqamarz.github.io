Privacy policy — TakingsJar

Last updated: 20 October 2026

NqApps, Sydney, Australia · sharmnadqam@gmail.com

TakingsJar counts notes and coins at the end of the day.

This app is a counting tool. It adds up notes and coins and shows the difference between that count, the float, and the POS cash total you type in. It does not give accounting, bookkeeping, or tax advice, and it does not calculate tax. Verify the result against your POS and your bank records.

On this device

Cash-ups, tills, staff initials, the reminder, the theme, the language, and the Pro flag stay on this device. Staff initials are optional and are at most four characters. The app has no account and no server, and it does not upload that data.

There is no automatic backup. Android backup is turned off, and the app excludes its files from cloud backup and device transfer. A backup is a JSON file you export yourself and share from the system share sheet. Restore uses the system file picker. You export and import manually. The app does not upload your backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device settings, to delete the cash-ups, tills, staff initials, and settings stored on the device. There is no server copy to delete. A backup file you already exported stays wherever you saved it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show AdMob ads. A banner can appear on History and on Settings only. There is no banner on the counting screen, and the banner is not placed next to the bottom navigation. An interstitial can appear only after a cash-up has been saved successfully, and at most once every three minutes. It is shown only when one is already ready. It is not shown while you are counting, before you export or share, while the share sheet is open, while the app is in the background, when the app launches, when you open the app from the daily reminder, or when you come back from the share sheet.

A PDF export on the free version asks first. The dialog says the reward is one PDF and includes “No thanks”. If you agree, one rewarded ad unlocks that one PDF. If no ad is available, that PDF is free that time, and the app says so. CSV export does not show an ad. Pro removes ads and includes unlimited PDFs.

Ad requests are non-personalised. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before any ad loads. Ads load only when Google reports that a request is allowed. Settings includes Privacy options when Google requires them, so you can change that choice.

AdMob may receive your approximate location and information about your interactions with the app, along with device and advertising identifiers, under Google’s own policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. This app does not send your cash-ups to an analytics or crash-reporting service. The app does not use Firebase Analytics.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store)

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or Apple. The store sets the price. Buying it removes ads and unlocks unlimited tills, unlimited history, and unlimited PDF exports. The free version has one till, 30 days of history on screen, and PDF export through the rewarded ad described above. Older cash-ups stay on the device. Restore purchases is on the Pro screen and in Settings. The store handles payment. This app does not receive your card number.

Notifications

The daily close reminder is a local notification scheduled on this device. It is inexact: the app does not use an exact alarm. The notification permission is requested only when you turn the reminder on, not when the app starts. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us.
