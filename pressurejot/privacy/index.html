This page describes the PressureJot app (Android package com.nqapps.pressurejot).

Health information and this app

PressureJot lets you record blood pressure readings, pulse, time of day, tags (for example "after coffee"), notes and an optional free-text medication note. This is health information. It is stored only on your device. NqApps does not collect, receive, see or store it, and it is never sent to our advertising partner.

PressureJot is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and it is for personal record-keeping only. Readings come from your own blood pressure monitor and are not checked by us. Always talk to your doctor about your readings. In an emergency, call 000 (or your local emergency number).

Exports and backups. If you create a PDF, a CSV or a backup file, it is saved or shared where you choose (for example, emailed to your GP or saved to a cloud drive). Once it leaves the app, that service's privacy terms apply. Think about who can access it.

Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Because this is a health-related app, we only request non-personalised ads. Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour. Even so, AdMob may collect device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings), your IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions. Google uses these to limit how often an ad repeats, count ad views, and prevent fraud (see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services). AdMob never receives your readings, tags, notes or medication notes.

Deleting your data. Settings → Delete all data permanently erases every profile, reading, range and reminder on this device, along with any export files the app still has in its temporary folder. You can also delete a single reading (in History) or a profile (in Settings). Uninstalling the app also removes the data, because the app turns off Android cloud backup and device transfer, and excludes its data from iPhone backups. Save a backup file first if you want to keep your readings.

Age. PressureJot is intended for adults (18+).

On-device data

Readings, profiles, your optional ranges, reminder times and settings stay on this device. There is no account, no sync and no Health Connect or Apple Health link. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own in the app. The Google AdMob SDK may collect diagnostic information about its own ads, as described above. The only network use is for ads and for store purchases.

Overseas. NqApps does not receive your readings, so they are not sent overseas by us. The ad and device information collected by Google AdMob (above) is processed by Google, which may store and process it outside Australia, including in the United States.

If you add a profile for someone else, only add their readings with their permission.

Backup

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app. On iPhone, the app excludes its data from iCloud and computer backups (Library, Preferences, Application Support, Documents and Caches). A backup file, PDF or CSV is created only when you ask for one, and goes only where you choose to share it. Backup files are not password-protected.

Ads

Banners appear on the History and Trends screens only, never on the screen where you enter a reading or the first-run screen. At most one full-screen ad is shown per day. It is never shown on your first day, never on the "Reading saved" screen, never within a few seconds of saving, and never when you open or leave the app. There are no rewarded ads. PressureJot Pro removes ads.

Before ads load, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool runs where Google requires it. Whatever you choose there, every ad request from PressureJot is non-personalised and carries no keywords or content links. Settings includes Ad privacy options when Google requires that choice. Ad content is limited to the MA rating or below, and the app is not directed at children.

Purchases

PressureJot Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). It removes ads and gives unlimited profiles and PDF exports. NqApps does not receive your card details. Purchases and refunds are handled by the store. This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer laws.

Notifications

Optional morning and evening reminders use notifications on this device. They contain no readings. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

Children

PressureJot is not directed at children. The intended audience is 18 and over.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy or the app limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer laws.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how your health information is handled, the app will show it to you before it applies.
