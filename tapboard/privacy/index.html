TapBoard privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

TapBoard is a custom soundboard made by NqApps. This policy describes what the Android app does with information.

Your recordings and imports stay on the device

TapBoard does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts or location. Sounds you record and audio files you import, along with your boards, tile names, colours, volume and trim settings, are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded, shared or sent to NqApps or anyone else.

The microphone is used only when you tap Record, to record the sound you choose, and never in the background. Imported files are chosen by you through the system file picker; TapBoard does not scan your storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not subscribe, the app shows ads through Google AdMob. After you answer Google's consent form where required, Google AdMob may collect and share the device advertising ID, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), IP address and diagnostic information (such as crash and performance data) to deliver, measure and improve ads and prevent fraud. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.

TapBoard is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children, and it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Remove ads is offered as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play Billing: weekly (A$0.99 per week) or monthly (A$3.49 per month), or the local price shown in Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; NqApps does not receive your card number or billing address. The app only checks with Google Play whether a subscription is active. There is no NqApps server.

Retention and deletion

NqApps does not run servers and does not keep copies of your data. Data stored on the device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept under Google's own retention policies; you can manage or delete it through your Google account at myaccount.google.com. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us at the address below.
