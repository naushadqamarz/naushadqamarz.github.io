NoiseTally privacy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

NoiseTally is a noise diary from NqApps, Sydney, Australia. Contact sharmnadqam@gmail.com. NoiseTally is not for children.

The app shows an approximate sound level from your phone's microphone while the meter screen is open. Sound is turned into numbers and discarded. NoiseTally does not record, save, or send audio. The microphone stops if you leave the meter, lock the phone, or take a call. Measuring pauses if the phone delivers no sound at all from the microphone (for example, another app takes it).

Reminders are scheduled on the device only and nothing is sent anywhere.

Diary

Diary entries, notes, times, annoyance, and optional photos are stored in the app. Photos are chosen with the system picker. Use a JPEG or PNG. Location details are removed from that photo before it is stored. HEIC photos are not stored. NoiseTally does not ask for your location, contacts, or camera permission.

You can delete every diary entry in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes the copy stored in the app. A backup file you already exported stays wherever you saved it.

Backup

Device backup does not include this diary. Export a backup file before you change phones. Android Auto Backup is off, and cloud backup and device transfer exclude the app's files. On iPhone, the diary files are excluded from iCloud backup. The backup file you export contains your diary, notes and photos. Store it somewhere private.

Ads on Android

The Android app can show ads from Google AdMob until you buy Pro. Those ads can use approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers, including the advertising ID, and can share them for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ads are always non-personalised. Settings includes Privacy options when a choice is required. The app does not send diary text, noise types, or notes to the ad network. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The iPhone app does not include an ads SDK.
