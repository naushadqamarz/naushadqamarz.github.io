Golf Scorecard privacy policy

Draft. "Golf Scorecard" is a working name.

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Golf Scorecard ("the app") is a Stableford golf scorecard that estimates a Daily Handicap from details you enter. It is made by NqApps, a seller based in Sydney, Australia. This page explains what happens to information in the app.

In short

  • There's no account or sign-in, and the app has no analytics of its own.
  • Your courses, rounds, scores, handicaps and player names stay only on your device. NqApps has no server and receives none of it.
  • There's no GPS: the app doesn't ask for your location.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Pro, a one-time purchase, removes them.

What you enter

You type in your club's card yourself (par, stroke index, slope and scratch rating), your GA Handicap and handicap category (Men/Boys or Women/Girls, used only in the Daily Handicap formula), and the scores of the players on your card. Playing partners' names are stored only on your device. They leave it only if you choose to share a scorecard image, and the image shows your own card unless you switch partners on. Partners then appear as initials with their Daily Handicap and points. Their GA Handicap never appears on the image. The app doesn't include or download any course database.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads on the History and Stats screens, plus a full-screen ad after a round is saved. When the app starts, Google's consent message (UMP) asks for your choices where the law requires it, and you can change them later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The app asks for non-personalised ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostic information so that it can serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ad categories such as gambling and betting are blocked.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store, and it doesn't renew. The store handles payment and NqApps never sees your payment details. The app only asks the store whether your account owns Pro, so that Restore purchases works.

Backup and restore

Backups are manual. Export backup creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet, so you decide where it goes. Restore reads a file you pick with the system file picker. The app never uploads your backups, and it asks for no storage permission. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for this app.

Accuracy

The Daily Handicap is an estimate, calculated on your phone with the published Golf Australia method from the details you enter. It isn't an official GA Handicap or Daily Handicap and may differ from your club's figure. For competitions, use the figure from your club, the Golf Australia app or GOLF.com.au. Scores in this app aren't submitted to Golf Australia or your club. The app is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Golf Australia, The R&A or the USGA.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting a round or course removes it straight away. Backup files you exported are under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
