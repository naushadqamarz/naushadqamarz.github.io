Dueandpaid privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Dueandpaid: Invoice Quote Bill (com.nqapps.dueandpaid) is an invoice, quote and bill tracking app made by Naushad Qamar (NqApps). This policy describes what the Android and iOS app does with information.

What Dueandpaid stores on the device

Dueandpaid is offline-first. It does not ask you to create an account and has no cloud sync. Your invoices, quotes, clients, bills and settings are stored only on your device in a local database. NqApps does not receive them.

If you choose a business logo, the app reads the photo you pick from your photo library and keeps a copy on the device only. It does not access other photos.

When you share or email an invoice or quote, the app hands it to your device's mail app or share sheet. The app itself does not send anything; whatever you share goes through the app or service you choose, under that service's own privacy policy.

Bill reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. Notification permission is used only for these reminders.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have an active subscription, the app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. After you answer Google's consent form where required, Google AdMob may collect and share the device advertising ID, device information, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), IP address and diagnostic information (such as crash and performance data) to deliver, measure and improve ads and prevent fraud. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Dueandpaid is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions (weekly, monthly or yearly) are auto-renewing in-app subscriptions through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the payment and renewals; NqApps does not receive your card number or billing address. The app only checks with the store whether you have an active subscription. There is no NqApps server.

Payments and advice

Dueandpaid helps you record and track invoices and bills. It does not process payments, move money, collect debts or give tax, legal or financial advice.

Retention and deletion

NqApps does not run servers and does not keep copies of your data. Data stored on the device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app; uninstalling deletes it. Back up anything you need before uninstalling. Data collected by Google AdMob, Google Play or Apple is kept under their own retention policies; for Google you can manage it at myaccount.google.com. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us at the address below.
