Privacy policy — Dhikr Goals

NqApps, Sydney, Australia · sharmnadqam@gmail.com

Dhikr Goals stores your counts, goals, streaks, reminders, theme and the remove-ads flag on this device only. The app has no account and does not upload that data.

On this device

Counts and settings are kept in local storage. A backup is a file you export yourself and share from the system share sheet. Restore uses the system file picker. Android Auto Backup is off (android:allowBackup="false"), and the app excludes its files from cloud backup and device transfer. There is no other backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show AdMob ads: banners on the dhikr list and on history only; at most one interstitial a day, and only after a counting session ends because the daily goal was completed; and a rewarded ad that unlocks extra themes. There is no banner on the counter. There are no ads on the morning or evening adhkar screens. An interstitial is never shown on launch, when you press Back, while you are still counting, or when the app was opened from a reminder.

Ad requests are non-personalised. In the EU and the UK, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before ads load. Settings includes Privacy options so you can change that choice where Google requires it. Ads load only when Google reports that a request is allowed.

AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers and diagnostics under Google’s own policy. Dhikr Goals does not send your dhikr counts or the text of your dhikr to an analytics or crash-reporting service. The app does not use Firebase Analytics.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase, dhikrgoals_remove_ads. The store sets the price. Buying it removes all banners, all interstitials and all rewarded prompts. Dawn and Night stay free. The other themes unlock with the purchase, because the rewarded prompt is removed. Restore purchases is on the Remove ads screen and in Settings. The store handles payment. This app does not receive your card number.
