Noon & Moon privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Noon & Moon is a puzzle game made by NqApps. This policy describes what the Android app does with information.

What Noon & Moon stores on the device

Noon & Moon does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Progress, the daily streak, settings (theme, sound, haptics, show mistakes), in-progress boards, and a local flag that ads have been removed are stored only on the device, with Android shared preferences. Uninstalling the app deletes that data. NqApps does not receive it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. After you answer Google's consent form, Google may collect and use the device advertising ID, IP address, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Noon & Moon is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children, and it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.

If you buy Remove ads, the app stops requesting ads. Google Play processes the payment. Noon & Moon does not receive your card number or billing address. The app only keeps a local note that the purchase is owned. There is no NqApps server checking receipts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
