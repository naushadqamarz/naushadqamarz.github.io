Noon & Moon privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

Noon & Moon is a puzzle game made by NqApps. It is distributed on Google Play and on the Apple App Store. The Android package name and the iOS bundle identifier are com.nqapp.noonmoon. This policy describes what the app does with information.

What Noon & Moon stores on the device

Noon & Moon does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Progress, the daily streak, settings (theme, sound, haptics, show mistakes), in-progress boards, and a local flag that ads have been removed are stored only on the device. On Android that storage is shared preferences. On iOS it is the app's local preferences. Uninstalling the app deletes that data. NqApps does not receive it and does not keep a copy on a server.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. After you answer Google's consent form, Google may collect and use the device advertising identifier, IP address, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

On iOS, ad requests are non-personalised. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google may still receive the advertising identifier (IDFA). When tracking is not allowed, iOS provides a zeroed identifier. Google may use that identifier, the IP address, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and limit fraud on ads. NqApps does not use this information to track you, and NqApps does not receive a copy of it.

Noon & Moon is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children, and it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.

Remove ads

If you buy Remove ads, the app stops requesting ads. On Android, Google Play processes the payment. On iOS, Apple processes the payment through In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Noon & Moon does not receive your card number, billing address, or other payment details. The app only keeps a local note that the purchase is owned. There is no NqApps server checking receipts.

The purchase can be restored from Settings on a later install of the same store account. Restoring asks Apple or Google whether that account already owns Remove ads. NqApps does not see the payment.

How long data is kept

Data stored by the app stays on the device until you uninstall Noon & Moon or delete the app's data. NqApps does not keep a separate copy, so we have nothing of yours to retain after that.

Advertising data is held by Google, not by NqApps. Google describes how long it keeps that data in Google's privacy policy. Payment records are held by Apple or Google under their own rules. NqApps cannot delete a payment record it never received.

How to ask for deletion

To delete progress, settings, and the local Remove ads flag, uninstall Noon & Moon. That removes the data from the device. A later install starts empty unless you restore the Remove ads purchase from the same Apple or Google account.

NqApps has no account to delete and no server copy of your puzzles. If you want to ask about advertising data Google holds, email sharmnadqam@gmail.com and we will point you to Google's request process. For an Apple or Google payment record, use that store's privacy tools. We cannot see or erase those records.
