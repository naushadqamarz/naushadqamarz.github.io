Privacy policy — DrawerTally

NqApps, Sydney, Australia · sharmnadqam@gmail.com

DrawerTally counts notes and coins at the end of the day. The name on your device is the name configured in the build.

This app is a counting tool. It adds up notes and coins and shows the difference between that count, the float, and the POS cash total you type in. It does not give accounting, bookkeeping, or tax advice, and it does not calculate tax.

Cash-ups, tills, the reminder, the theme, the language, and the Pro flag stay on this device. The app has no account and does not upload that data.

On this device

There is no automatic backup. Android Auto Backup is off (android:allowBackup="false"), and the app excludes its files from cloud backup and device transfer. A backup is a JSON file you export yourself and share from the system share sheet. Restore uses the system file picker. You export and import manually. The app does not upload your backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show AdMob ads. A banner can appear on History and on Settings only. There is no banner on the counting screen, and the banner is not placed next to the bottom navigation. An interstitial can appear only after a cash-up has been saved successfully, and at most once every three minutes. It is not shown while you are counting, before you export or share, when the app launches, when you open the app from the daily reminder, or when you come back from the share sheet.

A PDF export on the free version asks first. The dialog says the reward is one PDF and includes “No thanks”. If you agree, one rewarded ad unlocks that one PDF. CSV export does not show an ad. Pro removes ads and includes unlimited PDFs.

Ad requests are non-personalised. In the EU and the UK, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before any ad loads. Ads load only when Google reports that a request is allowed. Settings includes Privacy options when Google requires them, so you can change that choice. The in-app privacy label opens this page and does not print the address in the app.

AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers and diagnostics under Google’s own policy. This app does not send your cash-ups to an analytics or crash-reporting service. The app does not use Firebase Analytics.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store)

Pro is a one-time purchase, drawertally_pro_lifetime. The store sets the price. Buying it removes ads and unlocks unlimited tills, unlimited history, and unlimited PDF exports. The free version has one till, 30 days of history on screen, and PDF export through the rewarded ad described above. Older cash-ups stay on the device. Restore purchases is on the Pro screen and in Settings. The store handles payment. This app does not receive your card number.

Notifications

The daily close reminder is a local notification scheduled on this device. It is inexact: the app does not request an exact-alarm permission. The notification permission is requested only when you turn the reminder on, not when the app starts. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us.
