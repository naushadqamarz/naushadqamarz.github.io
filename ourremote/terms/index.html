1. What OurRemote does

OurRemote lets you use your phone as a Wi-Fi remote for TVs and streaming devices that run Google TV or Android TV OS, on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. You pair once with the code shown on your TV.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't work with other TVs, and it isn't a streaming service.

How it works: OurRemote uses the remote feature built into Google TV and Android TV devices. If a TV software update changes that feature, some buttons or the connection may stop working until we update the app. We'll try to fix problems quickly, but we can't promise that every TV model or software version will keep working.
