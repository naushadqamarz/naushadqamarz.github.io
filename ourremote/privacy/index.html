OurRemote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

OurRemote is a Wi-Fi remote app for Google TV and Android TV devices. It is made by Naushad Qamar, trading as NqApps, in Sydney, Australia ("NqApps", "we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use OurRemote on Android or iPhone.

The short version: OurRemote has no account, no ads, no analytics and no NqApps server. Your TV details and pairing keys stay on your phone. We don't receive them.

1. What stays on your phone

To find and control your TV, OurRemote uses and stores the following only on your phone:

  • TV discovery: the app looks for Google TV and Android TV devices on your home Wi-Fi using the phone's standard local network discovery (Bonjour/mDNS, service _androidtvremote2._tcp; on Android through network service discovery, NsdManager). It reads the TV's name, model and local network address (for example 192.168.1.40). You can also type the address yourself.
  • Local Network permission (iPhone): iOS asks your permission before the app can find and talk to devices on your home network. If you say no, the app can't reach your TV. You can change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Pairing keys: the first time you connect, your TV shows a code and the app creates a security key and certificate for that TV. They're stored encrypted on your phone (on Android, protected by the Android Keystore; on iPhone, in the iOS Keychain, set to this device only and not synced to iCloud) and used only to talk securely to your TV.
  • Your settings: your TVs, theme and other app preferences.
  • What you press and type: button presses and text you type on the app's keyboard go straight from your phone to your TV over your home network. They're not stored and not sent anywhere else.

None of this is sent to NqApps or to any other company. On Android, app data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iPhone, pairing keys stay in this device's Keychain and aren't synced to iCloud.

Removing it: choose Forget this TV in the app to delete that TV's details, pairing key and stored certificate from your phone. Uninstalling OurRemote deletes all its data from your phone. You may also want to remove the phone from your TV's own settings.

2. Purchases

OurRemote is free to use. Optional OurRemote Pro is sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time purchase. In the United Kingdom, only the one-time purchase is offered.

  • The store handles payment. Google (Google Play) or Apple (App Store) collects and processes your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details.
  • In the app: to unlock Pro, the app receives from the store, on your phone, the status of your purchase (for example "active" or "expired", with a purchase token or transaction record). This is used only to turn Pro on or off.
  • From the stores: Google and Apple give us sales reports (for example product, price, date and country, and on Google Play an order number) so we can keep business and tax records. If you contact us about a purchase, we may use your order number to help you. Apple handles all App Store refunds.

3. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you. We keep support emails for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer for a legal claim. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), so messages are stored on Google's servers.

4. What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No account, sign-in or NqApps server.
  • No location access.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal information.

5. Network permissions

OurRemote needs network access (on Android, network permissions; on iPhone, the Local Network permission) to find and connect to your TV on your local Wi-Fi. It talks only to TVs on your network and, for purchases, to Google Play or the App Store.

6. Children

OurRemote is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Australia

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). In practice we hold very little: support emails and store sales records.

  • Access and correction: you can ask to see or correct personal information we hold about you by emailing us.
  • Complaints: please email us first. We'll reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you may be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at https://www.oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.
  • Overseas storage: support emails are stored by Google, which may store data outside Australia.
