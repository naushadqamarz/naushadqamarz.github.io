Privacy policy — PutAwayJar

Effective date: 21 October 2026

NqApps / Naushad Qamar, Sydney, Australia · sharmnadqam@gmail.com

PutAwayJar is a savings tracker. You mark amounts you put aside. The app does not hold or move money.

Not financial advice

Not financial advice. PutAwayJar is a general information and savings-tracking tool. Amounts and goals you enter are stored only on your device and are used only to show your own progress. The app does not consider your objectives, financial situation or needs, and nothing in it is a recommendation about any financial product. PutAwayJar doesn't hold, move or invest money and isn't a bank or financial service. Before making financial decisions, consider whether they're appropriate for you and think about getting advice from a licensed financial adviser (you can check an adviser on ASIC's Financial Advisers Register).

On this device

Challenges, the amounts you mark, the reminder time, the theme, and the purchase flag are stored only on this device. The app has no account and no server, and it does not upload that data. There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

A backup is a manual JSON file you start yourself. You choose where that file goes, through the system share sheet. Restore uses the system file picker and is also started by you. The app does not upload your backup. CSV export is a separate file and is part of the one-time purchase.

Android system cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"), so app data is not included in device backups. The app also sets android:dataExtractionRules so Android 12 and later exclude every domain from cloud backup and from device transfer, and android:fullBackupContent so older Android versions exclude all app data from a full backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device settings, to delete the challenges and settings stored on the device. There is no server copy to delete. A backup file you already exported stays wherever you saved it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show Google AdMob ads. A banner can appear on the challenge list and on the progress screen, with a gap above the bottom navigation. It is not on the badges screen, the period grid, settings, or the new-challenge screen. An interstitial can appear only after Mark saved, and at most once a day. It is shown only when one is already ready. It is not shown when the app launches, when you open the app from a reminder, while the share sheet or file picker is open, or while the app is in the background.

Two rewarded ads are optional. One unlocks one extra challenge. One unlocks the other two themes for 24 hours. Each dialog states the reward and has Cancel. If no ad is available, or you close it first, nothing is unlocked. The one-time purchase removes ads, removes the challenge limit, unlocks every theme, and includes CSV export.

Ad requests are non-personalised. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before any ad loads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Ads load only when Google reports that a request is allowed. You can change that consent via Privacy options in Settings when Google requires them.

AdMob and UMP may collect device identifiers, an advertising ID, an IP address, ad interaction, and coarse location derived from IP. See Google’s privacy policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

The paid unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play or Apple. The store sets the price. We do not receive your payment details. Restore purchases is in Settings and on the purchase screen. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store, together with your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

Notifications

Notifications are local reminders only. They are scheduled on this device and are inexact: the app does not use an exact alarm. The notification permission is requested only when you turn reminders on, not when the app starts. Nothing about the reminder is sent to us. The app still works if you decline.

Children

PutAwayJar has no children-directed content. It is not directed at children.
