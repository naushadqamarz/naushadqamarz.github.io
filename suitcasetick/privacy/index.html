SuitcaseTick privacy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

SuitcaseTick is a packing checklist from NqApps, Sydney, Australia. Contact sharmnadqam@gmail.com.

SuitcaseTick is for a general audience aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. The kids and baby template packs are for adults packing for children.

What stays on your phone

Trips, lists, templates, reminder times, and settings stay on the device. SuitcaseTick does not ask for your location, camera, or contacts, and it has no account. A purchase flag is stored on the phone so Pro stays unlocked after you buy it.

Android backup is turned off, and the app data directory is excluded from iCloud backup. A backup file you export yourself is the only way to copy your lists to another phone. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone.

Sharing a list uses the system share sheet. The app can write a temporary text or PDF file for that share, then deletes the file afterwards. You choose where the share goes.

Reminders

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules an inexact notification for the evening before you leave. It does not ask for an exact-alarm permission.

Ads on Android

The Android app can show ads from Google AdMob until you buy Pro. The iPhone app does not include an ads SDK.

On Android, Google (the ad partner) may receive an advertising or device identifier, and the kinds of information Google describes for ads, such as approximate location, interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Google uses that to show ads, measure them, and reduce fraud. The connection is encrypted. SuitcaseTick does not send your lists, templates, photos, contacts, or messages with an ad request. Those stay on the phone. Uninstalling the app removes the app's data from the phone.

Ads are requested as non-personalised. Google may still use identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. Where required, for example in the UK and EEA, a consent form is shown before ads load. On Android you can change that choice in Settings, then Privacy options.

The app does not send trip names, lists, or template text to the ad network. Google describes how it uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

SuitcaseTick Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play or the App Store. It removes ads on Android and unlocks every template pack. Refunds are handled under the store's policies. This doesn't limit your rights under consumer law, including the Australian Consumer Law and UK consumer law.

Complaints

Write to sharmnadqam@gmail.com first. If you are in Australia and you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles.

SuitcaseTick does not check the weather, airline baggage rules, or travel requirements.
